Minka provides the technology layer for organizations to securely publish and transfer money and balances on the web. Minka's core product is the Minka Ledger, a unified transaction model designed to allow diverse companies to communicate and exchange value using a common language and a small set of foundational primitives.

The Ledger acts as the engine upon which Minka builds specialized solutions, such as Real-Time Payments (RTP) and Payments Hubs, enabling organizations to manage complex financial ecosystems.

## Network Payments

Minka facilitates network payments by ensuring that money and balances published through one Minka Ledger can be moved securely to any other connected Ledger in real time. The platform guarantees this by using digital signatures for every transaction, establishing trust and non-repudiation (the inability to deny that a transaction occurred).

## Fundamental Principles of Minka Solutions

Minka builds all solutions on a set of core principles that govern security, reliability, and flexibility. These principles ensure the platform is both trustworthy for financial operations and adaptable for diverse use cases.

### Data Integrity and Trust

The Minka Ledger ensures that all transaction records and balances are accurate, secure, and tamper-proof.

- **Mechanism:** The platform achieves this integrity by implementing cryptographic chaining (blockchain principles) to secure transaction history within a centralized, auditable system.
- **Auditability:** Every operation is cryptographically signed and verified, creating a complete and immutable audit trail.

### Transactional Atomicity

The platform enforces reliability at the transactional level to prevent partial operations.
- **Intent and Claims:** Every transaction is submitted as an Intent, which packages one or more individual actions known as Claims.
- **Guarantee:** The Intent ensures atomicity (all associated Claims must either all succeed or all fail together). This guarantee prevents situations where one part of an operation (like a debit) completes while another part (like a tax collection) fails.

### Configuration and Adaptability

Minka's solutions are designed for structural flexibility, allowing you to tailor the platform to your specific business and regulatory needs.

- **Purpose:** Configuration refers to the structural setup and adaptability of the system, enabling you to customize and manage your deployed financial solutions.

- **Customization:** You define operational rules, compliance requirements, and external connections (via Bridges) to customize the Ledger for specific regional or business requirements.
