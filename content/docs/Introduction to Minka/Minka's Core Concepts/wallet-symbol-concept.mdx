---
title: Wallets and Symbols
---

These two concepts are fundamental building blocks of the Minka Ledger: Wallets are the accounts that hold balances, and Symbols define the unit of value (asset or currency) being held.

## Wallets

A Wallet represents an account or participant in Minka's finalcial ecosystem and adapts to the use case and organizational layer (bank, enterprise, etc.) in which it operates.

Wallets enable the creation, transfer, and management of virtual funds between different entities using the Minka ledger platform. 

<Callout type="note">
  They are not tied to a particular user interface or technology but underpin the movement of balances regardless of whether access is through CLI, API, or UI Studio. 
</Callout>

## Symbols

A Symbol represents a specific asset type or unit of value that can be held and transacted within Wallets. The system's flexibility is derived from the fact that Symbols are designed to track far more than just real-world currency.

<Callout type="note">
 This allows the system to remain flexible and supports diverse applications beyond standard financial currency tracking.
</Callout>

### Use cases

The distinction between "Symbol" and "Currency" allows the Minka Ledger to support diverse applications beyond traditional finance:

- Monetary Examples: A Symbol can be a traditional fiat currency (like USD or Euros) or a virtual currency (like USDT).

- Non-Monetary Examples: The concept of Symbol is used when building systems such as loyalty programs, where the tracked unit might be a loyalty point count, gift card value, or credit score rather than an official currency.
