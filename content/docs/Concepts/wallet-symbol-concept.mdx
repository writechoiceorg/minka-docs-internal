## Wallets

A Wallet represents an account or participant in Minka's finalcial ecosystem and adapts to the use case and organizational layer (bank, enterprise, etc.) in which it operates.

Wallets enable the creation, transfer, and management of virtual funds between different entities using the Minka ledger platform. 

<Callout type="note">
  They are not tied to a particular user interface or technology but are abstract objects that underpin the movement of balances regardless of whether access is through CLI, API, or UI Studio. 
</Callout>

## Symbols

A Symbol represents a specific currency or asset type that can be held and transacted within wallets on the ledger and is designed to track more than just real-world monetary units.

<Callout type="note">
 This allows the system to remain flexible and supports diverse applications beyond standard financial currency tracking.
</Callout>

### Use cases

While most use cases currently involve traditional currencies (like USD, euros, or pesos), the concept is valuable for non-currency units.

The distinction between "symbol" and "currency" facilitates the building of systems that are not solely focused on currency, such as:

- Financial Examples: A symbol could be USD (US Dollar), USDT (Tether), or any other fiat or virtual currency the ledger supports.

- Non-Financial Examples: You use the concept of Symbol when building systems such as loyalty programs, where the tracked unit might be a loyalty point count rather than an official currency.
