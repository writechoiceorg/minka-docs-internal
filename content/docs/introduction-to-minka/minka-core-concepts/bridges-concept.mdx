A Bridge is a configuration record in the Minka Ledger that acts as the essential connector between your internal client systems and the core payment network. It is the necessary mechanism for extending the Ledger's functionality to interact with external services, such as bank cores or centralized payment networks.

## Integration Workflow

For participants, the Bridge is the first technical touch point for integrating with the system.

To successfully integrate a Bridge follow the process below:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Registration
You must register your Bridge, which you can typically do quickly through the dashboard. This associates your external service with a secure configuration record in the Minka Ledger.
</Step>

<Step>
### Implementation
You must then develop and configure your external Bridge service. This involves setting up the necessary API endpoints on your end and implementing Minka's security protocols for signing the payloads that are sent to and from the Ledger.
</Step>

<Step>
### Defining API Interaction
Once your Bridge is implemented, the Ledger can use it to securely interact with your external service. The primary operations a Bridge enables are:
    - **Debits:** Instructing your external system (e.g., a bank core) to debit an account.
    - **Credits:** Instructing your external system to credit an account.
    - **Intent Status Notifications:** Receiving status updates from your Bridge (e.g., completed, failed) about the state of a transaction.
</Step>
</Steps>
